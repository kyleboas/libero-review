Cookie policy

Updated 9 November 2026

This site is Kyle Boas's private review page for Libero Football. It sets one cookie, and only if you ask it to.

The cookie

It's called libero_review_key. If you tick "Keep me signed in on this device for 7 days" when you enter the password, it saves a key made from the password so you don't have to type it again. It isn't the password and it says nothing about who you are.

It expires 7 days after you sign in. It only works on this site and is only sent over a secure connection.

Nothing else

No analytics, ads or tracking, and nothing is shared with anyone.

Hosting

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Your browser sends the cookie to GitHub along with each request for the site, as it does with any cookie, but the site itself never reads it on a server. GitHub may keep records like IP addresses for security. See GitHub's privacy statement.

Removing it

Click Sign out at the bottom of the review page, or clear cookies for kyleboas.github.io in your browser. If you'd rather not have it at all, untick the box when you sign in. The site works the same either way.
