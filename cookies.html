Cookie policy

Libero Football review site · kyleboas.github.io/libero-review · last updated 9 November 2026

This site is a private, password-protected review page for Libero Football, run by Kyle Boas (Libero Games). It uses one cookie, and only if you ask it to.

The cookie we use

Namelibero_review_key
What it doesKeeps you signed in, so you don't have to type the password again on this device.
What it holdsA key worked out from the password, which unlocks the page in your browser. It does not hold the password itself or anything about you.
When it is setOnly when you sign in with "Keep me signed in on this device for 7 days" ticked.
How long it lasts7 days from when you sign in. After that you enter the password again.
TypeStrictly necessary and first-party: it only does the job you asked for. It is limited to this site's address, sent only over secure connections (Secure) and never with requests from other sites (SameSite=Strict).

What we don't use

No analytics, advertising, tracking or social media cookies, and no other cookies or browser storage. We don't build a profile of you or share anything with third parties.

Our host

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. The page is unlocked in your browser; the site does not read the cookie on a server. As with any cookie, your browser sends it along with requests for this site's files. GitHub may keep records such as IP addresses for security; see the GitHub General Privacy Statement.

How to remove it

  • Choose Sign out at the bottom of the review page, which deletes the cookie straight away.
  • Or delete cookies for kyleboas.github.io in your browser's settings.
  • To not set it at all, untick "Keep me signed in" when you enter the password. The site works the same either way.

Changes

If this changes, we'll update this page and the date at the top.
